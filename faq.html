A few helpful details before you book a cleaning service with SAHLAN Premium Cleaners.

What cleaning services do you offer?

SAHLAN Premium Cleaners lists domestic cleaning, commercial cleaning, post-construction cleaning and fumigation services.

How much does a cleaning service cost?

Pricing depends on the service, property, size, condition and scope. Share the details of your space using the quote form so the team can discuss a tailored price with you.

How do I book?

Call 0711 224 477 or use the quote form on the Contact page. The form opens WhatsApp with your details filled in for you to review and send.

Do you cover my area?

Please contact the team with your location to confirm service availability before making plans.

Can I request a specific date?

Yes, you can include your preferred date in the enquiry. The date is a request and must be confirmed with the team.

What should I share when asking for a quote?

Tell us the service you need, your location, the type and size of the space, your preferred timing and any special requirements.

Is fumigation safe for people and pets?

Safety instructions depend on the specific treatment and provider. Before booking, ask the team to explain preparation, precautions and re-entry guidance. Always follow the treatment provider’s instructions.
